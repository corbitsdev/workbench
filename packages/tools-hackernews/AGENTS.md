# @workbench/tools-hackernews

HackerNews search and story tools. Registered in the hub's tool registry as `hackernews_*`.

- Keyless — uses the public HackerNews Algolia API; no credential required and no seed entry needed
- The tool grants are synthesized at session launch from the agent's capabilities list; do not add them to the DB
- Keep the tool schemas in sync with what agents declare in their system prompts
